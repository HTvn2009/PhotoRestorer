Help

Short instructions written like a classroom handout.

Add a name, upload a JPG, PNG, or WEBP file, write any known context, then run restoration. After the result is ready, save it to My Gallery or share a saved project with a link.

Use real photographs with a clear main subject. Avoid screenshots, cartoons, AI images, or files larger than 10 MB.

Write only information you already know, such as a person, place, date, event, object name, or family story. Leave the field blank if you are unsure.
